Evidence

Every line on the map is graded

A lot of online trees are copied from each other, mistakes and all. I don't add a parent unless the records support it. If a record names them, the link is documented. If the evidence is strong but indirect, I mark it probable. On the map, documented links are solid lines and probable ones are dashed.

What DNA says

From AncestryDNA ThruLines, as of 27 Oct 2026. So far the DNA doesn't contradict anyone in the tree. Each bar counts cousins who share DNA with me and descend from the same couple through a different child. Those are the matches that count as independent evidence.
